# RepoFinder product demo V3

## Story in one sentence

RepoFinder turns a vague idea for `letsgochristo.com` into a focused, low-friction experiment by combining live repository evidence with a conversation about the constraint that matters most: ease of use.

## Run time

Target: 90 to 95 seconds.

## Scene 1, the idea

**Time:** 0:00 to 0:11

**On screen:** The live `letsgochristo.com` home page. A yellow note points to the hero and says, “Goal: add an AI video avatar.”

**Narration:**

“This is my portfolio. People can read about my work, but I want them to meet me without booking a call. The goal is simple: add an AI video avatar.”

## Scene 2, give the idea context

**Time:** 0:11 to 0:23

**On screen:** The real RepoFinder form filled with `https://letsgochristo.com` and `AI video avatar`. Yellow outlines identify the site, the goal, and the Find repos button.

**Narration:**

“I give RepoFinder the live site and the outcome I want. That context matters. I am not searching for the most famous avatar project. I am asking what fits this website.”

## Scene 3, reveal the tradeoff

**Time:** 0:23 to 0:37

**On screen:** The first real result card for Duix-Avatar. The star count, ease score, and GPU requirement are called out. A yellow note says, “Powerful is not the same as easy.”

**Narration:**

“RepoFinder searches live GitHub data and uses OpenAI to rank the options. Duix-Avatar rises to the top with fourteen thousand stars and full control, but its low ease score reveals the catch: I would need GPU infrastructure.”

## Scene 4, add the constraint the ranking cannot know

**Time:** 0:37 to 0:50

**On screen:** The real RepoFinder chat over the avatar results. The user asks for easier options and says paid products are acceptable.

**Narration:**

“That is where the demo becomes a decision tool. I open the result and add the constraint the rankings could not know: I care more about ease, and I am willing to pay.”

## Scene 5, watch the answer change

**Time:** 0:50 to 1:06

**On screen:** The real chat answer recommending HeyGen, Synthesia, and Captions, with a yellow outline around the assistant answer.

**Narration:**

“The answer changes. RepoFinder recommends hosted choices, explains when HeyGen, Synthesia, and Captions fit, and brings the advice back to my actual site. It even asks the next useful question: polished videos, or a real-time avatar?”

## Scene 6, turn the answer into an experiment

**Time:** 1:06 to 1:18

**On screen:** A four-step diagram: need, constraint, choice, test. The selected path is a HeyGen prototype on one portfolio page.

**Narration:**

“Now I have a focused experiment. Start with HeyGen on one portfolio page, test whether visitors watch or engage, and only then decide whether real-time interaction is worth the added complexity.”

## Scene 7, one capability for humans and agents

**Time:** 1:18 to 1:30

**On screen:** A simple diagram. A human uses the browser and conversation. An agent calls `recommend_repos` through MCP. Both reach the same recommendation engine.

**Narration:**

“A person can explore this in the browser and refine the answer through conversation. An agent can call the same recommendation engine through M C P and use structured results inside its own workflow.”

## Scene 8, the value

**Time:** 1:30 to 1:40

**On screen:** The journey resolves to one line: `letsgochristo.com + AI video avatar → HeyGen prototype`. Three value statements appear below it.

**Narration:**

“That is the value of RepoFinder: less tab hopping, fewer wrong-fit integrations, and a faster path from an idea to a test you can actually run.”

## Voice direction

- Confident and conversational.
- Read at 150 to 160 words per minute.
- Pause briefly after “Powerful is not the same as easy.”
- Say “M C P” as three letters.
- Do not generate audio until the script and storyboard are approved.

## Accuracy notes

- `letsgochristo.com` is the live portfolio used in the screenshots.
- The recommendations and follow-up answer are real RepoFinder output captured on August 4, 2026.
- Duix-Avatar was the first result in the captured run, with about 14,300 GitHub stars and a visibly low ease score.
- HeyGen, Synthesia, and Captions came from the real follow-up chat response.
- The HeyGen prototype is the proposed next experiment, not a completed integration.
